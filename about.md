## Harm Behrens

Business and technology director with **25+ years** of building and operating
**large-scale internet platforms** - from startups and publishing houses to one
of the largest ecommerce groups worldwide. Dipl.-Ing. Industrial Engineering
(TU Berlin), working in **German, English and Spanish**, based in **Hamburg**.

### How I work

* Deliver the **"right" software** pragmatically - prototype first, then commit.
* Develop teams towards an **agile, test-driven** mindset and **continuous
  delivery**, so that change stays cheap.
* Treat **AI readiness** as a leadership topic: training, shared coding
  instructions, TDD-prompting, agents for the boring parts.

### Stations

* **Chief Technology Officer**, ePages GmbH, Hamburg - since 2014.
  Agile development, maintenance and operation of a multi-tenant SaaS
  ecommerce platform; teams in Hamburg, Jena, Barcelona and Bilbao.
* **Division Manager E-Commerce**, Otto GmbH & Co. KG, Hamburg - 2010 to
  2014. Development and operation of the Otto group web shops and a SOA-based
  middle layer.
* **General Manager**, FuchsCom GmbH - 2006 to 2010. Business and product
  management of the internet portals tarif-fuchs.de, mail-fuchs.de,
  iByCall.de, online-fuchs.de.
* **Interim Manager / General Manager**, mobilcom AG, INFOnline GmbH,
  AGIV AG - 2003 to 2005. SEO, UX optimisation, publishing systems:
  ad server, CMS, image database.
* **General Manager**, DOCK23 - Bauer Internet Company - 1999 to 2002.
  Set up and grew the internet service provider of the Bauer Publishing Group.
